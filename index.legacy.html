What it can't know.

Three things Shopify's API genuinely does not expose. Profitkit models them and labels every modelled figure, so you always know which numbers are firm.

Actual shipping cost
Not in the API at all — not null, absent. It's your figure, or an estimate.
Fees outside Shopify Payments
No gateway but Shopify's reports what it charged. Everything else is modelled from rates you set.
Why something was refunded
Only refunds processed through Shopify's Returns flow carry a reason. Refunds issued from the order page carry none.
Orders older than 60 days
Shopify's standard order permission stops there. History builds forward from the day you install.
